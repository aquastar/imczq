**NSF REU opportunity**

I am excited to announce a summer research opportunity through the NSF Computer and Information Science and Engineering (CISE) Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) program.

**What is REU?**

The REU program provides undergraduate students with hands-on research experience in cutting-edge topics in computer science and engineering. This is an excellent opportunity to:

* Work on meaningful research projects in machine learning, graph neural networks, and AI
* Gain valuable experience for graduate school applications or industry careers
* Receive a competitive stipend rate for your 10 week summer work (up to 40 hours per week)
* Collaborate with faculty and graduate students
* Potentially contribute to publishable research

**Eligibility**

* Undergraduate students
* U.S. citizen or permanent resident
* Strong interest in computer science research
* No prior research experience required - enthusiasm and willingness to learn are what matter most

**What to Expect**

* Duration: Typically 10 weeks during summer 2026
* Format: Full-time research commitment
* Mentorship: Close guidance from faculty and graduate students
* Deliverables: Research presentation and/or written report at program conclusion
*
